A few pages to begin with

A thought to keep

Even the quietest things are growing.

Like a garden after rain, we can be changing in ways no one sees. These pages are for the in-between moments: the almosts, the goodbyes, the tiny beginnings, and the feelings that take their time becoming words.

with tenderness, always.
Behind the pages

Hi, I’m the heart behind these words.

This is a little home for my poems—words about growing, family, faith, longing, and the feelings we sometimes keep hidden. Some poems help me understand myself; others help me let go. I hope you find a piece of yourself in them, too.

Every poem comes from a different corner of the heart, and every page holds something worth keeping.

“May these words find the people who need them, exactly when they need them.”
